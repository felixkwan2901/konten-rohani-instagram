# Style research: @diam.dan.percaya ("Tenang"), January 2027 (W15–W18, 3–30 Jan)

Research date: 22 October 2026. The goal is 48 new procedural styles (40 images at 1080×1350 and 8 Reels at 1080×1920). They come from studying 50 design-led Instagram accounts.

**Ground rules followed**
- Other accounts are used for style ideas only. No content, logos, characters, mascots or brand marks are copied. Wherever a brand has a recognisable mark (the Origami USA boat, the Pantone wordmark, the Duolingo owl, the Poetry Foundation pegasus), the concept avoids it on purpose.
- Every concept can be drawn with Pillow + numpy: shapes, gradients, noise, halftone and simple line drawing. No photos, no AI images, no complex illustration.
- The concepts only use fonts from the allowed macOS list.

**How the accounts were studied (honesty note)**
- Each profile was opened logged out at `instagram.com/<handle>/` in a browser pane, without logging in, accepting anything or downloading. Logged-out Instagram shows the profile header, bio, highlight covers and the latest ~9–12 posts. I looked at those grids as screenshots at reduced size. I did not open individual posts or carousels.
- **"seen"** in the table means I actually looked at that account's grid. When a borrowed element was *not* among the visible posts, the cell says so (for example "seen; signal flags inferred"). I did not use any account I could only read about.
- **Handles that failed and are not counted:**
  - Not available: @experimentaljetset, @thetypecooker, @monocle, @soulsgrowndeep, @batikkeris, @sejauhmatamemandang, @modernquiltguild, @mccallpatterns, @interfaceingame.
  - Wrong account: @hey_studio, @pentagram, @teenage.engineering, @nasamoon, @kurz_gesagt, @playdate, @tulisan, @sejauhmata.
  - Impostor or fan accounts: @informationisbeautiful, @bibleproject.
  - Private: @transitmap.
- **Viewed but left out of the table** because their grids were almost all photo or video stills with nothing drawable: @notionhq, @nasa, @oatly.

**What the 50 have in common, and what differs from @diam.dan.percaya's 158 styles**
1. **Objects with a job.** The strongest posts show a real object with a function: a chip card, a matchbox, a specimen box, an element tile, a flag. Tenang already does this well (receipt, ticket, telegram). The gaps are maritime objects, tools, textiles, puzzles and scientific objects.
2. **Systems over pictures.** The data and type accounts (Lupi, Fragapane, OWID, Google Fonts) make beauty out of a *system*: a grid, a ramp, a flow, a specimen. These systems are cheap to draw procedurally.
3. **Generative craft.** Hobbs and Lieberman show that numpy-style fields (flow, rings, refraction) can look premium. MARMER, SIDIK, RIAK and SPIRO bring this in.
4. **Indonesian vernacular.** Batik, congklak, hand-painted roadside signs and *not angka* hymnals are almost absent from the Christian accounts I looked at, including Indonesian ones like @renunganalkitab, which post photo plus text. This is an open lane for Tenang.

## Part 2: 48 new concepts

### Shared drawing conventions (all concepts)
- **Canvas:** images `W, H = 1080, 1350`; Reels `RW, RH = 1080, 1920` at 30 fps through `cerita.ffmpeg_writer(out, RW, RH, FPS)`.
- **Existing helpers to reuse:** `hn`, `georgia` (gaya_w6); `ft`, `ease`, `fade`, `sprite` (gaya_w7); `avenir`, `bulat` (SF Rounded), `spidol` (Bradley Hand), `charter`, `goresan`, `kurva`, `catmull`, `ayat_tengah` (gaya_w14); `render.fbm2d`, `render.grain`, `render.wrap`.
- **Font files:**
  - `/System/Library/Fonts/`: Avenir Next.ttc (idx 0 Bold, 2 Demi, 5 Medium, 7 Regular, 8 Heavy), HelveticaNeue.ttc, Menlo.ttc, MarkerFelt.ttc, Noteworthy.ttc, SFNSRounded.ttf.
  - `Supplemental/`: Futura.ttc, Didot.ttc, Baskerville.ttc, BigCaslon.ttf, Iowan Old Style.ttc, Charter.ttc, GillSans.ttc, Rockwell.ttc, AmericanTypewriter.ttc, Courier New*.ttf, DIN Condensed Bold.ttf, Impact.ttf, Arial Black.ttf, SnellRoundhand.ttc, Bradley Hand Bold.ttf, Chalkboard.ttc, SignPainter.ttc, Trattatello.ttf, Georgia*.ttf.
- **Glyph coverage (tested here, 22 Oct 2026):**
  - Greek and Cyrillic: Georgia, Helvetica Neue, Avenir Next, Baskerville, Iowan, DIN Condensed, Rockwell, American Typewriter, Menlo, Courier New. Not Futura, Didot or Gill Sans (Gill Sans has Cyrillic only).
  - Hebrew: only Courier New. Pillow has raqm, so use `direction="rtl"`.
  - `→`: only Courier New and Menlo. `✓ ★`: only Menlo.
  - Everywhere else, **draw arrows, ticks and stars as polygons.** Test a glyph with `bytes(f.getmask(ch)) != bytes(f.getmask("\U000F0000"))`.
- **Shared text helper:** several concepts need text set on a circle. Write one helper `teks_lingkar(img, cx, cy, r, text, font, fill, start_deg)` that pastes per-character sprites rotated to the tangent, and reuse it in SIDIK, SILSILAH, RIAK and FASE.
- **Finishing:** every image ends with the handle (`@diam.dan.percaya`) and the reference. Print-like styles get `grain(img, 0.2–0.5)`. Hairline-heavy concepts (SIDIK, MUARA, SPIRO, BENANG) render at 2× and downsample with LANCZOS.
- **Content rules:**
  - These follow riset/SPEK_W15.md and are repeated in every content module.
  - Verses are TB word for word.
  - Every reference to God is capitalised (Tuhan, TUHAN as in TB, Allah, Yesus, Bapa, Aku/-Ku, Engkau/-Mu, Dia/-Nya).
  - Inside parables TB keeps "aku/ia" lowercase for the landowner or shepherd; do not change TB text. Capitalise only in our own lines.
- **Verse check:** every sample verse below was looked up in TB (alkitab.sabda.org). None is used in `konten/tenang_w7.py … tenang_w15.py` (`tenang_w15.py` appeared during this research and was checked too, 22 Oct 2026). No reference repeats inside a week or across the 48. Re-run `cek_modul.py` when each week's module is written.
- **Psalm numbering:** for some chapters the SABDA API shows a Hebrew/English cross-number such as "(31-17)" (e.g. Mzm 31, Yes 9). The samples avoid those chapters; keep TB numbering.
