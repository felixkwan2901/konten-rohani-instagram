# Brag Plan: diam.dan.percaya — web arsip

## What is this app?
A quiet web archive of every @diam.dan.percaya devotional post (378 images and reels in a draggable photo sphere, grouped into series 01–05 and Reels), plus two small games: Roti Hidup (break bread, get a random TB verse) and Stand Firm (tower defence).

## The angle
The followers already know the posts; what they don't have is a place to find them again. Open on that feeling ("the verse that held you last week — can you still find it?") and answer it with the real site, filmed on a phone.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen, Playfair serif: "Ayat yang menguatkanmu minggu lalu…" then "masih bisa kamu temukan?" The question is personal and specific to the account.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real sphere revealing "Be Still and Trust" and being dragged around; "378 postingan" counts up.
- Folder chips switching 02 → Reels, then the grid of sections.
- Tapping the loaf in Roti Hidup: it breaks, crumbs fly, a TB verse appears.
- Stand Firm: defenders placed, first wave marching.

## Outro / punchline
"Coba websitenya sekarang" + kwanfelix.me/diam.dan.percaya + "Link di bio".

## User flow worth showing
Open the site → drag the sphere / switch a series → break the bread and receive a verse.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet devotional product film, Instagram Reel
- Interpretation: few words per scene, long holds, soft crossfades, one phone frame that stays put while the footage changes inside it.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: ~33s (user asked for 30s–1 minute, so this overrides the usual 15–25s)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #000 with a faint warm glow rgba(201,139,74,.10) (Roti Hidup page)
- Accent: #f0d3a3 (bread crumb / verse reference colour)
- Text: #f4f2ef, dim #8c8783
- Display font: Playfair Display 400
- Body font: Inter 300/400
- Strongest visual element: the photo sphere with "Be Still and Trust" in its centre

## Share copy (draft)
Semua renungan @diam.dan.percaya sekarang ada dalam satu web — putar arsipnya, pecahkan roti, terima satu ayat. Link di bio 🤍

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed
- Music: output/musik/fajar.wav (the account's own hopeful piano, made by musik.py, safe for a business account)
- Music treatment: fade in 0.6s, steady low-mid level, fade out over the last 1.8s
- Music cue guidance: no bundled preset for this track; piano with soft attacks, so natural timing is used for readability
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (restraint suits the devotional tone)
- SFX posture: sparse — soft clicks on the two chip taps, a soft drop when the bread breaks, one gentle select on the CTA
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, no hits on text

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3.4s
Black. Two Playfair lines arrive one after the other and hold.
Sequential/interaction: yes — line 2 arrives 1.2s after line 1
Audio intent: piano starts, nothing else
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The sphere — 7.0s
Phone frame rises in; inside: real recording of the intro veil lifting onto the sphere and a drag. Top: kicker "@diam.dan.percaya", title "Semua renungan, dalam satu bola foto.", counter "378 postingan".
Sequential/interaction: real drag in footage; counter counts 0 → 378
Transition mood: soft crossfade inside the phone → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Series — 6.8s
Footage: chips 02 → Reels tapped, menu → The Archive grid. Top: "Tersusun per seri." + chip row 01 02 03 04 05 Reels staggered in, then held.
Audio-coupled idea: soft click on each real chip tap
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Roti Hidup — 7.0s
Footage: loaf tapped, breaks, TB verse appears; second loaf. Top: kicker "Roti Hidup", "Pecahkan roti, terima satu ayat.", small "Alkitab TB".
Audio-coupled idea: soft drop when the loaf breaks
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Stand Firm — 4.5s
Footage: defenders on the road, wave 1 marching. Top: kicker "Bonus", "Main Stand Firm."
Transition mood: phone shrinks away → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — CTA — 4.8s
Wordmark diam.dan.percaya, "Coba websitenya sekarang", URL pill, "Link di bio".
Audio-coupled idea: one gentle select as the URL lands; music fades out
